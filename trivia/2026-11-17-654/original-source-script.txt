1 Point Per Correct Answer ~ Joker Is NOT In Play

Allow one minute between each clue ** Only one guess per team for the entire round.
Wrong answers are zero points. 
Remind the audience that the famous person can be real or fictional, living or dead.
On each clue remind the audience that you are still looking for the same famous person.

Round 5 Intro:
Attention, gumshoes! Carmen Sandiego has vanished into the night, and it’s up to you to determine where she’s hiding.
We are looking for one famous city, which can be real or fictional. You will receive five clues about Carmen’s location, worth 10, 8, 6, 4, and finally 2 points.
You may turn in only one answer during the entire round. Once you submit a guess, you are locked in—and a wrong answer is worth zero points. We are looking for the same place after every clue.

10 points: Our agents first spotted Carmen on the grounds of King’s Inns, admiring the “Hungry Tree”—a tree that has grown around an iron bench and appears to be eating it. Where in the world is Carmen Sandiego?
8 points: Disguised as a street musician, she was next seen on Grafton Street, a filming location for Once, the musical romance featuring the Oscar-winning song “Falling Slowly.”
6 points: Disguised as a student, Carmen entered Trinity College, where the medieval manuscript known as the Book of Kells is displayed.
4 points: She then visited Windmill Lane Recording Studios, whose famous clients include U2. Unfortunately, our agents still haven’t found what they’re looking for.
2 points: This is your final clue, gumshoes! Carmen was last seen enjoying a pint at the Gravity Bar atop the Guinness Storehouse in the capital of Ireland. Where in the world is Carmen Sandiego?
Dublin, Ireland
