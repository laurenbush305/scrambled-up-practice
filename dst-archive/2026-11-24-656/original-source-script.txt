Round 3 –Florida Man or Florida Fiction?
1 Point Per Correct Answer ~ Joker Is IN PLAY.
I'll read eight outrageous headlines from Florida. Some are based on real news stories. Others are completely made up. Your job is to determine: Florida Man or Florida Fiction?
1. A Florida man is arrested after attempting to pay his speeding ticket with 14 pounds of frozen shrimp.
Fiction

2. A Florida man with a tattoo of Florida on his forehead is arrested after calling 911 for a ride home.
Florida Man (In 2021, 22-year-old Matthew Leatham was arrested after calling 911 to ask for a ride home.)

3. A Florida man tries to rob a bank using a handwritten note on the back of his own utility bill.
Fiction

4. A Florida man calls 911 to report a drunk driver, then admits the drunk driver is himself.
Florida Man (Michael Lester called 911 on New Year's Eve in 2017 to report his own impaired driving.)

5.  A Florida man drove onto a closed beach and straight into the ocean, later telling cops he wanted to surf, and it wasn't his fault that the "truck don't surf.”
Florida Man

6. A Florida man is arrested after attempting to return a lawn mower he stole to Home Depot for store credit.
Florida Fiction

7. A Florida man calls 911 to report his cocaine stolen.
Florida Fiction (But a Florida man did once called 911 to ask police to verify if the meth he just bought was real, leading to his arrest when it tested positive)

8. A Florida Man buys a mask at a gas station to burglarize the bank across the street.
Florida Man
