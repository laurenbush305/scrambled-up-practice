Round 6 – Wave Until You’re Blue
2 Points Per Correct Answer 
From the ocean to your radio, this round explores different ways to make waves.
1. Keanu Reeves goes undercover among surfers to catch a gang of bank robbers in what 1991 action movie?
Point Break

2. “Heat Waves” became a major hit for what British band whose name sounds like a fragile zoo exhibit?
Glass Animals

3. Godzilla is known for creating what kind of destructive ocean waves usually caused by an undersea earthquake?
Tsunami

4. Sound waves need something to travel through—which helps explain the tagline “In space no one can hear you scream.” What 1979 movie used it?
Alien

5. Krazy George Henderson led a nationally televised stadium wave for the first time at an Oakland A’s–Yankees playoff game in what decade?
The 1980s (1981!)

6. In radio broadcasting, FM stands for “frequency modulation.” What does AM stand for?
Amplitude modulation

7. By bouncing sound waves off underwater objects, what technology helps submarines detect things?
Sonar

8. What high-fidelity streaming music service did Jay-Z buy in 2015 and relaunch?
Tidal
